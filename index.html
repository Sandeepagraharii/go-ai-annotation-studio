💡 GO-AI Studio Guided Walkthrough

1. Task Queue (Left Sidebar) Select any of the 8 simulated fulfillment center tasks (infeed tables, sortation chutes, mobile stow pods) or upload your own image.
2. Annotation Canvas (Center) Click and drag with your mouse (or touchscreen) to enclose items. Use hotkeys 1 to 6 to rapidly set package labels.
3. Quality Attributes & Review (Right) Mark checkboxes for Occluded (partially hidden), Truncated (frame edge), or Damaged. Press Enter to submit.
4. IoU Automated Audit & Analytics Get instant quality scoring against mathematical ground truth. Visit QA Dashboard to see trend charts, error distributions, and export official audit reports.
